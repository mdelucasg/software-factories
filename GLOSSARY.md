# Cadence

An online course platform where instructors publish courses and students purchase and take them, individually or through team seats.

## Language

### People & Roles

**Student**:
A user who enrolls in and takes courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: Learner

**Buyer**:
A user who pays for a purchase, either for their own enrollment or for team seats.
_Avoid_: Purchaser

**Instructor**:
A user who creates and teaches courses on Cadence.

**Staff**:
The people who speak for and manage a specific course—either the course's owning instructor or a platform admin.

### Purchases, Access & Teams

**Purchase**:
A payment for a course, made either for the buyer's own enrollment or for one or more team seats.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Enrollment**:
A student's active access to a course, granted either by a direct purchase or by redeeming a coupon, and revoked if the purchase is refunded.

**PPP (Purchasing Power Parity)**:
A country-based discount tier applied at checkout that ties course access and coupon redemption to the country of purchase.

**Team Seat**:
One unit of course access bought as part of a team purchase.
_Avoid_: Team licence

**Coupon**:
A single-use invite code generated for each team seat that grants one student enrollment in a course (not a price discount).
_Avoid_: Discount code, promo code

**Redeem**:
To use a coupon to enroll in a course and occupy a team seat.
_Avoid_: Claim

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A buyer's one-time request to have a purchase refunded, reviewed by course staff and moving from **Requested** to either **Approved** or **Declined**.
_Avoid_: Reversal request, pending chargeback, cancellation request, open refund, pending refund

**Refund**:
The full return of the amount originally paid for a purchase after its refund request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback, cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The 14-calendar-day period from the purchase date during which a buyer may submit a refund request, provided course usage stays under the watch-time and lesson-completion limits and no coupons from the purchase have been redeemed.
_Avoid_: Cancellation window, reversal window
